Character portrait checker

Check whether each character and coach has a reachable online portrait. Anime profile pictures come from AniList or MyAnimeList when available. A Haikyuu Wiki thumbnail may be manga artwork. When none is available, the game displays its original illustrated backup.

This checks unique characters. Their School, Nationals, Timeskip, Elite and Pro cards reuse the character's portrait unless variant-specific artwork is supplied. Not every manga-only or very minor character has an official anime portrait.
